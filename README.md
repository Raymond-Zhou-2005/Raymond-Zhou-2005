# Raymond (Ruimeng) Zhou

**Applied mathematics, biomedical AI, and reproducible computational research**

I am an undergraduate at **Duke Kunshan University**, Class of 2027, studying applied mathematics and computational sciences with a mathematics focus. My academic experience also includes coursework at **Duke University**.

My work connects mathematical modeling, medical image analysis, evidence-grounded AI, and public health. I build research workflows in which assumptions, source data, experiments, and software outputs can be inspected together. Alongside research, I have worked in industry analysis, teaching and peer support, and student-led community initiatives.

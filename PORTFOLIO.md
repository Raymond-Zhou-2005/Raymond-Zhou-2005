## Research and scientific software

### Structured PCV vaccination modeling

**Research manuscript and open-source computational framework**

Developed a framework for vaccination-program analysis that connects continuous-time Markov dynamics, cohort ordinary differential equations, and age–calendar transport formulations under matched conditions. The work combines mathematical derivations with separately evaluated pneumococcal-vaccine product scenarios.

- Organized **144 scenario configurations**, traceable parameter and source ledgers, retained simulation outputs, and figure-to-script mappings.
- Prepared a research manuscript, six main figures, two main tables, and a versioned reproducibility archive.
- Documented solver checks, accounting consistency, portability diagnostics, and interpretation boundaries.

The PCV application is a methodological and conditional scenario study. Its computational checks are distinct from empirical calibration and clinical or policy validation.

[Code and documentation](https://github.com/Raymond-Zhou-2005/pcv-structured-model) · [Aligned v1.0.1 release](https://github.com/Raymond-Zhou-2005/pcv-structured-model/releases/tag/v1.0.1)

### Radiotherapy Physics RAG and evidence workflows

**Open-source retrieval software and related research prototypes**

Built local evidence-retrieval tools for question answering over radiotherapy physics reports, with report-aware retrieval, structured citations, provenance metadata, and an MCP interface.

- Developed workflows for document parsing, chunking, retrieval, navigation, and evidence inspection.
- Organized reproducible evaluation procedures and source-grounded output contracts.
- Extended the research toward bounded literature-workflow execution, evidence ledgers, failure retention, and recovery.
- Investigated limitations in automated scoring, including cases in which surface evidence matching does not capture factual polarity.

The public QA software and later research prototypes represent different development stages. Automated benchmark results are research measures rather than clinical validation; the controlled research-workflow evaluation did not establish superiority over its literature-workflow baseline.

[Public QA repository](https://github.com/Raymond-Zhou-2005/radiotherapy-physics-rag)

### Protein-corona and molecular data engineering

**Data integration, molecular processing, and research-quality control**

Developed traceable workflows for protein-corona research by harmonizing five source collections while retaining assay, species, sample-role, and measurement distinctions.

- Organized **1,346 source-sample records** and source-specific protein or protein-group information.
- Processed a molecular-structure collection containing **50,547 records**, including **10,962 phospholipid records**.
- Implemented structural fragmentation and reconstruction checks, source identity tracking, and explicit handling of unresolved mappings.
- Preserved distinctions between identification-only records and quantitative abundance measurements.

This work focuses on data engineering and structural quality control. Harmonized identifiers do not imply that all measurement scales are directly comparable.

## Research practice

I aim to make research reusable through clear problem definitions, source and parameter provenance, versioned code, retained failures, documented computational checks, and readable technical communication. I distinguish a working implementation from a validated scientific claim, and a research manuscript from a peer-reviewed publication.

**Research interests:** biomedical AI; medical image processing; evidence-grounded language-model workflows; mathematical public-health modeling; health economics; numerical methods; reproducible scientific computing.

**Tools:** Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib, R, tidyverse, Quarto, Git, and domain-specific imaging and circuit-design tools.
